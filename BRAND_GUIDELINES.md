## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Purpose

**ImadLabs** is a premier software development studio engineering modern desktop applications, media streaming utilities, high-throughput download managers, and productivity tools.

### Core Brand Pillars
- **Software Development Expertise:** Precision-crafted software with architectural integrity and clean code.
- **Technology & Innovation:** Pushing boundaries with multi-threaded performance and contemporary user experiences.
- **Reliability & Quality:** Rock-solid stability, zero bloat, high security, and offline reliability.
- **Speed & Agility:** Fast download engines, instant-launch utilities, and responsive interfaces.
- **Scalability:** A unified design architecture that seamlessly expands across every new product.

## 2. Official Visual Identity: The HexaCore Matrix

The official brandmark for ImadLabs is **The HexaCore Matrix** (Approved Concept 02 Refined).

### Geometric Construction & Anatomy
- **No cube:** The mark is flat and has no box, cube or container, so it can sit on any background and be resized freely. Never add the old isometric cube back.
- **The Quantum Dot ("i"):** A glowing Quantum Core dot (`#00FFC2` with a `#FFFFFF` center) that forms the tittle of the letter **"i"**.
- **The Kinetic "L":** A thick, rounded Cyber Mint (`#00F5A0` to `#00D285`) stroke that drops straight down and sweeps right to form the letter **"L"**, with a thin white highlight running through it. Together with the dot it reads as **"iL"**, for ImadLabs.
- **Glow:** On dark backgrounds the dot and L carry a soft mint glow. On light backgrounds the L runs from mint to Electric Cobalt (`#0066FF`), with no glow.
- **The Wordmark:** Typographic lockup set in customized **Plus Jakarta Sans** with geometric kerning. `Imad` is rendered in pure white (dark mode) or deep slate (light mode), while `Labs` is accentuated in Cyber Mint (`#00F5A0`).

## 5. Logo Clear Space & Minimum Sizes

### Clear Space Rule
The minimum exclusion zone around any ImadLabs logo lockup is defined as **$1.0X$**, where $X$ is the diameter of the **Quantum Core Dot**.

### Minimum Reproduction Sizes
- **Digital Screens (Standalone Symbol):** `16 × 16 px` (Use simplified vector variant [`brand/logos/imadlabs-logo-simplified-small.svg`](brand/logos/imadlabs-logo-simplified-small.svg)).
- **Digital Screens (Horizontal Logo):** `120 px width`.
- **Print Collateral (Symbol):** `5 mm × 5 mm`.
- **Print Collateral (Horizontal Logo):** `25 mm width`.
